## Voice

Describe how you write. Not how you want to write. How you actually write when you're at your best.

Think about: sentence length, rhythm, how you transition between ideas, whether you use metaphors or stay literal, how technical you go, how much you trust the reader.

### Core Rules

These are your non-negotiables. Things your writing always does or never does.

Start by reading your own past writing (not AI-assisted). What patterns do you see?

Examples of the kind of rules that work:
- "Short sentences. Fragments are fine."
- "No em dashes. Use periods."
- "No transition words between paragraphs. The next idea should follow without a bridge."
- "Name concepts. Don't describe them with a full clause when a two-word label works."

## How to Build This Skill

1. **Start with your own writing.** Read 10-20 things you wrote by hand (no AI). Blog posts, emails, Slack messages, tweets. Look for what's consistent.
2. **Name your anti-patterns.** Not your aspirations. What do you always delete? What makes you cringe when AI writes it?
3. **Capture the delta.** Every time you rewrite an AI draft, save both versions. The gap is the voice.
4. **Separate your audiences.** You don't write the same way to your team and your customers. Encode both.
5. **Keep calibrating.** This is a living document. Every time you catch a new pattern, add it.
