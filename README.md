# Lucy's voice skill

Anne-Marie's (Lucy's) writing voice, as a skill her bots load whenever they write, prep, or review something for her.

## What's inside

**[SKILL.md](SKILL.md)** is the filled-in skill. It covers:

1. **Purpose and limits**: what bots use it for, and what they may change in her own drafts.
2. **Voice**: how she writes, with a real example.
3. **Core rules**: plain English, name the mechanism, sources for numbers, and the rest.
4. **Use sparingly**: patterns that are fine now and then, not banned.
5. **Stories**: how she tells them, and why bots give her dated facts instead of inventing scenes.
6. **Channels**: LinkedIn, blog, newsletter digest, internal writing (documents, PR descriptions, issues), Slack and email.

**[SKILL-TEMPLATE.md](SKILL-TEMPLATE.md)** is the original empty template, kept for reference.

## Who uses it

Any bot that writes for her: posts and digest prep, reviews of her drafts, and internal communication such as documents and PR descriptions, where it applies.

## Keeping it current

Change the skill through a PR so she can review it. Keep it short: rules that recur, not one-off examples.
